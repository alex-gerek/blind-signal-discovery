# Saved 96/98 Hz example: before and after clustering

Scope: seed 22101, unit 0, saved 500 ms normalized excess-power maps only. All local maxima were enumerated across the full frequency axis before evaluation at known frequencies. Peak enumeration has no calibrated two-source decision rule; it is descriptive. Raw powers, complex STFT coefficients, local background estimates and shorter-scale maps were not saved for this example.

At all nine frame centers 1.25..2.25 seconds, the 96 and 98 Hz cells exceed Z=4 and belong to the same eight-connected component (label 21 in the close-mixture map). There is at most one strict local frequency maximum within 94..100 Hz per frame: 96 Hz in the first six frames, 100 Hz at 2.0 s, 98 Hz at 2.125 s, and 96 Hz at 2.25 s. No two resolved carrier-associated peaks with a sampled valley are present in this range. This narrow range is an evaluation description, not an input to the peak enumeration.

Example at 1.625 seconds: Z(94,96,98,100 Hz) is approximately (9.99,68.11,67.96,12.11). Two adjacent high cells are not two local maxima. Adjacent 2 Hz samples cannot themselves provide an intervening sampled valley. Ridge broadening and time variation are observable, but neither uniquely establishes two components: noise, taper, modulation or a single nonstationary source can also change them. The single-tone map itself has incidental local noise peaks.

The close-mixture connected component has 39 cells, center frequencies 94..104 Hz, raw score 693.703031754036 and standardized score 43.589948611960786, above threshold 5.869277578934621. Reconstructed accepted cluster sizes and scores agree with saved candidates for all four retained maps. It survives score acceptance as one component. It was not two accepted components rejected only by the final expanded-box rule. The normalized representation already lacks a clearly separated sampled double peak at the carrier positions; thresholding and connectivity then yield one component. No causal claim is made that thresholding alone erased a resolvable pair.

Only one close-mixture map was saved, not maps for all 100 units. All-unit accepted candidates exist, but cannot recover the rejected components or pre-cluster maps. Recomputing the other units requires the pinned generator/dependencies and parameters, verifying waveform hashes, then recording per-scale complex STFT, raw power, spectral/temporal background estimates, Z, seed mask, all labeled components before/after size filtering, standardized scores, rejection reasons and final boxes. For this saved map, Z>4, components and scores can already be reconstructed without waveform recomputation. Original phase information cannot be reconstructed from Z alone.

This inspection does not prove that the waveform contains no usable two-tone information. A phase-aware model or an untested temporal diagnostic may extract information not represented by separate power peaks. No new waveform experiment was performed.
