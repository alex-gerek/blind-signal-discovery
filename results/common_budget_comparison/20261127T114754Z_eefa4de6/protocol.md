## Estimand and common budget

One false-alarm WINDOW at most per nonoverlapping4s observation. Budget alpha_max=.01/window, equivalent to9 false-alarm windows/hour of generated-window exposure. This is a research comparison budget, not an operational astrophysical event FAR. Same raw inputs for both arms, independent splits for development/calibration/evaluation. Do not force equal observed alarm counts by retuning after evaluation.

The target distribution is an explicitly chosen IID mixture: one-third stationary pink noise, one-third smooth amplitude drift, one-third within-window level step. Each observation independently draws one regime and a fresh noise base. It is not a physically continuous time series or an adaptive regime test. Budget assurance applies to this overall mixture, NOT separately to every regime or to arbitrary real noise.

- For each arm compute noise count/3600 and exact95% two-sided Clopper-Pearson interval; its upper bound is also a97.5% one-sided bound. Using the two upper bounds gives a separate at-least95% joint evaluation confidence statement for the two fixed methods. - Evaluation budget-supported flag requires the upper bound<=.01 for BOTH arms. Report conditional regime rates/CIs descriptively; the overall guarantee does not imply conditional guarantees.
