## 3. Methodological Scope Limitations

- The 50,000-window confirmatory run tested a single synthetic configuration and failed to reject its specified null model. This does not prove that the entire detector is error-free or establish false-alarm control on non-stationary real data.
- Conformal guarantees (`P(score > threshold) <= alpha`) assume exchangeability; Sidak union budgeting assumes independence or positive orthant dependence across channels.
- Unphysical time-slides evaluate STFT peak coincidences only and do not recompute time-domain cross-correlation across shifted windows; background significance for physically confirmed (`xcorr`-verified) coincidences remains `UNAVAILABLE`.
- The reserved O3 holdout (`docs/v03_o3_holdout_manifest.json`) remains completely untouched (`0` O3 files accessed).
